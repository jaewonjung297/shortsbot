# shortsbot – Terms of Service

_Last updated: November 18, 2026_

shortsbot is a personal, open-source tool that generates short animated videos and, when the account owner
asks it to, sends them to that owner's own TikTok inbox as drafts. It is operated by its author for their own
channel and is not offered as a service to the public.

1. **Use.** Only the owner of a connected TikTok account may authorise shortsbot for that account. By
   authorising it, you allow shortsbot to upload draft videos to your TikTok inbox. shortsbot never publishes
   anything on your behalf; you review, edit and post each draft yourself in the TikTok app.
2. **Your content.** You are responsible for the videos you choose to post and for following TikTok's
   Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
3. **Revoking access.** You can revoke shortsbot's access at any time in TikTok under
   Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
